# Privacy Policy for Chronarch

**Effective Date: August 28, 2026**

Chronarch ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Chronarch mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Chronarch is designed with privacy as a priority.**

* **Personal Information:** We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
* **App Data:** The App stores information you create while using its features, such as timer sessions, focus points, rewards, and history. This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.
* **No Server Storage:** Chronarch does not operate a server or backend that receives or stores your App data.

## 2. Permissions and Internet Access

Chronarch does not require access to sensitive device features such as your camera, microphone, or location.

The App does not require an Internet connection and does not transmit your App data over the Internet.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

All data created within the App remains on your device. Because we do not receive or store this data, we cannot access or recover it.

We recommend using the built-in security features of your device, such as a passcode or biometric authentication, to help protect the information stored on your device.

If you uninstall the App or clear its data through your device settings, data stored by the App, including your session history and earned focus points, may be permanently deleted.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Chronarch does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or similar services.

We do not share your App data with third parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Chronarch does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because the App does not collect personal information, children can use the App without providing personal information to us.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and publish the updated Privacy Policy at this URL.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
